# Module Specification

## Clinical
Patients, EMR, odontogram, perio, diagnoses, procedures, notes, prescriptions, treatment plans, consent, imaging.

## Operations
Appointments, chairs, queues, doctors, staff, inventory, procurement, laboratory.

## Finance
Estimates, invoices, payments, refunds, expenses, reports.

## Engagement
Patient portal, reminders, recall, WhatsApp/SMS/email, campaigns.

## Intelligence
AI copilot, voice documentation, patient summary, treatment explanation, scheduling suggestions, inventory forecasting, analytics.

## Administration
Organizations, branches, roles, permissions, audit logs, configuration, backups.
