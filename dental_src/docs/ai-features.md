# AI Features

## AI Clinical Copilot
Draft SOAP notes, summarize history, prepare patient-friendly explanations.

## Voice Dictation
Speech → structured draft → clinician review.

## AI Receptionist
Appointment FAQs, availability and booking workflows with escalation for clinical emergencies.

## Treatment Assistant
Explain approved treatment options and compare plans using configured clinic data.

## Imaging Assistance
Potential findings with confidence/evidence UI; never autonomous diagnosis.

## AI Analytics
Revenue, chair utilization, no-show, treatment acceptance, inventory and recall insights.

All clinical AI outputs must remain drafts until authorized clinical review.
